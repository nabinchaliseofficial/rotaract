President's Message

Service Above Self

"Service Above Self" is more than a motto - it's the heartbeat of our club. As we embark on this new Rotary year, I am inspired by the dedication of every Rotaractor who steps up to serve our community. Together, we will build bridges, create opportunities, and leave a lasting impact in Galkot Baglung. Let's make this year one of fellowship, growth, and meaningful change.
Rtr. Nabin Chalise
President, R.Y. 2026-27
— Nabin Chalise

Privacy Policy

Rotaract Club of Galkot Baglung, RI District 3292

This policy explains what information we collect through this website (rotaractgalkot.org) and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you submit voluntarily, such as through the newsletter subscription form or membership inquiries (e.g. name, email).
  • Basic, non-identifying analytics about how visitors use the site (pages viewed, general location, device type).

How We Use It

  • To send club updates, event announcements, and newsletters to subscribers who opt in.
  • To respond to membership or contact inquiries.
  • To improve the website and our communications.

How We Protect It

Member and subscriber data is stored in our secured database and is not sold to third parties. Access is limited to authorized club officers.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time by emailing us. You may also request that we delete your data by contacting the club.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to galkot@rotaract3292.org.
